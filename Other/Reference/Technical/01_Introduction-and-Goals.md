# 1. Introduction and Goals

## Overview

OpenIMS claims to be a useful inventory-management-system.

Its inventory-model represents the physical world: locations, the buildings in them, the areas (rooms of a building, or areas like a yard), the containers in the areas (cupboards, boxes, ...) and the items in them.
For every item OpenIMS knows where it belongs to (its target place) and where it actually is (its real place).
Access to the inventory is granted by assigning groups to inventory-objects; the access applies to everything which is contained in such an object as well.
See [the solution strategy](./04_Solution-Strategy.md#inventory) for details.
